## **_GRENG JAI_**


In Thailand, _greng jai_ (sometimes transliterated _kreng jai_ ) is the feeling of
being reluctant to accept another’s offer of help because of the bother it
would cause them.


_See also_ : GRATITUDE; _OIME_ .
