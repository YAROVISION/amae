## **_KAUKOKAIPUU_**

Sometimes we feel homesick for a place, even though we’ve never been
there. Sometimes we just want to be anywhere but here. From _kauko_ –
faraway, and _kaipuu_ – a yearning, the Finns know the craving for a distant
land as _kaukokaipuu_ (pronounced _ka_ -oo-ko _-kye_ -poh).


_See also_ : DISAPPEAR, the desire to; HOMESICKNESS; WANDERLUST .
