## **_NGINYIWARRARRINGU_**


Certain emotions end up such an important part of how we experience the
world that we unpick their subtle variations, christening each one. In the
West in the last ten years, we have named many types of anger ( _see_ : RAGE ).
For the Pintupi, whose homeland is the deserts of Western Australia, there


are fifteen different kinds of fear. Among them, _ngulu_ is the dread you feel
when you believe another person is seeking revenge; _kamarrarringu_ the
tense, frozen feeling when you sense someone is creeping up behind you;
_kanarunvtju_ a terror about bad spirits visiting in the night, so pervasive that
it stops you sleeping; and _nginyiwarrarringu_ is a sudden spasm of alarm
that makes a person leap to their feet and look about them, trying to see
what caused it.
